# Benchmarking

Use multiple benchmark types.

## Exact-answer benchmarks

Good for:

- MCQ;
- arithmetic;
- extraction;
- short factual answers.

Use exact or regex grading.

## Open-ended benchmarks

Good for:

- planning;
- comparison;
- RAG design;
- critique;
- policy review.

Use pairwise or rubric-based grading. Do not judge by exact match.

## Equal-budget comparisons

Compare fusion against the best single model with the same or similar model-call budget.
